About YACPOM Ventures

Built on Trust, Integrity, and Superior Engineering

Under the visionary leadership of CEO Charles Ampomah, YACPOM Ventures has established itself as a premier civil engineering and construction company situated in Labone, Accra.

We specialize in structural developments, complex demolition projects, heavy civil engineering, and specialized roofing solutions. Our multi-disciplinary team brings precision, safety compliance, and sustainable methodologies to every site we execute across Greater Accra and nationwide.
